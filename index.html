Part A — Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Shree Hari AI Micro Coach CS ("the App") is developed and published by Chandra Shekhar ("we", "us"), an independent developer based in India. For any privacy request or question, contact: jmd9cs@protonmail.com.

2. Data we collect: none

We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell, rent, share, or process any personal data or usage data from the App. Specifically, the App has:

  • No account or registration. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifier.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising SDKs. No Firebase, no ad networks, no crash-reporting services, no third-party code that phones home.
  • No server. We operate no backend. There is nowhere for your data to go.
  • No internet permission. The released Android app does not declare the INTERNET permission. This is not merely a promise — it is a technical property of the app: it cannot open a network connection.
  • No recording of any kind. The App never creates an audio file and never saves sound. It can convert your speech to text for immediate feedback, but that text is never saved and never sent to us. See §4.

3. Data stored on your device only

To function, the App saves the following locally on your device: your chosen language, focus areas, difficulty, coach tone, Sankalp setting, reminder time, whether the speaking features are switched on, plain numbers about how you spoke (for example seconds spoken, number of pauses, words per minute, filler rate, accuracy percentages), and your daily mission history (which mission, completed or skipped, and streaks). This information never leaves your device, is never visible to us, and is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption).

4. The speaking features — the optional microphone

The App includes optional speaking features. They are switched off by default and operate only if you turn them on yourself and grant the microphone permission. Everything below applies only while a speaking mission is actively running — never in the background.

4.1 The Bol-Meter. While you speak, the App measures how loud the sound around you is, in order to sense whether you are speaking, brighten the flame as you speak, and record how long you spoke. This is loudness detection only — no words are involved.

4.2 Speech to text. To give you feedback on your English, the App asks your phone to convert what you said into text, using your device's own speech-recognition service. Depending on your device, its settings and the languages installed, that conversion may happen entirely on your phone, or your phone's speech service may perform it online. That behaviour belongs to your device and to the provider of its speech service, and is governed by their terms, not ours. The App itself has no internet permission and sends nothing anywhere.

4.3 What is done with the text, and what happens to it. The recognised text is used immediately, on your device, to show you what you said, to count words, pauses, filler words and speaking speed, to compare your reading against a given sentence, and to point out a few common English patterns. It is then shown to you and discarded.

What this means precisely:

  • No recording. No audio file is ever created by the App, on your device or anywhere else.
  • No transcript is stored. The words you spoke are never saved by the App — not in your history, not in your progress, nowhere.
  • Only numbers are kept. The App stores plain figures on your device — for example seconds spoken, longest unbroken stretch, number of pauses, words per minute, filler rate, accuracy percentage — never the words themselves.
  • Nothing is sent to us. We operate no server and receive nothing. The App declares no internet permission.
  • Only while a mission runs. The microphone opens when a speaking mission begins and is released the moment it ends, is completed, is skipped, or you leave the screen.
  • Fully optional. You may decline the microphone permission or switch the speaking features off at any time; every mission can always be completed by hand, and the App works fully without them.
  • Feedback is limited and honest. Speech recognition is imperfect, and the App checks only a fixed list of common English patterns. It is a practice aid, not a language assessment.

5. Permissions the App uses, and why

  • Notifications — solely to show the one daily reminder at the time you choose. You may decline this permission or disable notifications at any time; the App works fully without it.
  • Microphone (optional, off by default) — used only for the speaking features described in §4, and only while a speaking mission is running. No audio is recorded or saved, and no text is stored. You may decline this permission; the App works fully without it.

The App requests no other permission: no camera, no location, no contacts, no call logs, no SMS, no file storage access.

6. Deleting your data

You are in complete control:

  • Tap "Erase all my data" in Settings — everything is permanently deleted immediately; or
  • Uninstall the App — Android removes all of its stored data.

Because no copy ever exists outside your device, there is nothing further for us to delete, and no deletion request to us is necessary — though you are welcome to write to us with any question.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience self-improvement tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because the App collects no data from any user, it collects no data from children.

8. Data protection laws

The App is designed around the strictest reading of data-minimization principles, including those underlying India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU GDPR: the simplest way to protect personal data is to never collect it, and that is what we do. Sound sensed by the microphone is processed ephemerally and is never retained by the App or sent to us; the text your device produces from your speech is likewise used once and discarded, and only plain numbers remain on your device. Where your device's own speech service performs the conversion online, that processing is carried out by your device provider under its own privacy terms. Since no personal data is processed by us, rights requests (access, correction, erasure, portability) are satisfied by design — your data exists only in your hands.

9. Third-party services

The App embeds no third-party service, no analytics and no advertising. It does use one facility of your own phone: its speech-recognition service, described in §4, which is provided by your device manufacturer or operating-system provider and governed by their privacy terms. If you obtained the App through Google Play, Google's own store services (downloads, updates, reviews) are governed by Google's privacy policy, which is independent of the App.
